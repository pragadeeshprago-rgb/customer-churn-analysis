How to read these KPIs

Definitions used in this dashboard

Churn rate = churned customers ÷ all customers × 100.

Retention rate = retained customers ÷ all customers × 100.

Revenue at risk = sum of monthly charges for churned customers in the filtered data. It is a proxy for lost recurring revenue, not a forecast.

Average tenure = mean number of months customers have stayed.
